# What Was Done Today - 2026-10-31

- Started `/weeklyannounce`, which schedules the weekly winners and announcements post for 5 minutes after the next clan competition ends, so WOM's final totals are in.
- Added a Templar-only `/weeklyannounce setup` command that only loads when `TEMPLAR_ROLE_ID`, `WOM_GROUP_ID` and `ADMIN_LOG_CHANNEL_ID` are set, so it fails closed.
- Gave it a required `channel` option only when the new `WEEKLY_ANNOUNCE_CHANNEL_ID` isn't set, and checked the bot can see the target channel and has Send Messages and Attach Files there.
- Gave it an optional `start` send time read in Eastern Time with the `/weeklycomp` parser, now exported from `weeklycomp.js`, which must be in the future. Left blank, it sends 5 minutes after the next competition to end in the WOM group, listing every competition that ends then.
- Added `findNextEnding` to `src/utils/weeklyAnnounce.js`, with a 20-second time limit on the WOM call.
- Verified the changes with `node --check` and scripted tests with Discord and WOM simulated, covering a non-Templar, unreadable and past start times, a typed start, a missing permission, channels the bot can't see, two competitions ending together, none ending in the future and WOM being down.
- Fixed the `/weeklycomp` start date parser (also used by `/weeklyannounce`) reading the early hours of a daylight saving change day an hour off, e.g. `2026-11-01 3` as 2am EST instead of 3am EST, by reading the time zone offset again at the converted time. An hour skipped when clocks go forward (2am on a spring change day) resolves to 1am. Checked every hour of 2026 and 2027 reads back as the hour typed.
- Made `/weeklyannounce setup` save a draft to `data/weeklyannounce.json` and privately show a builder panel with the target channel, the send time, an `N / 2,000` character count for the announcements and a fixed "Weekly winners, top 3 with chart" row naming the competitions it will use. The panel's side stripe turns amber with a ⚠️ once the announcements pass 2,000 characters.
- Listed each announcement on the panel with its number, first line as a title, character and line count, and Edit, Up, Down and Remove buttons, with Up disabled on the first row and Down on the last. Kept it to 5 announcements so every row fits in one message, with "Add announcement (n/5)" disabled at 5. Preview is shown but disabled until the preview stage.
- Made Add and Edit open a pop-up with one 2,000-character text box, Edit pre-filled, keeping the text exactly as pasted so Discord formatting, emojis and raw pings are posted as written. Titles on the panel never ping.
- Carried the draft ID in every button and pop-up ID and read drafts from disk on every press, so the panel keeps working after a restart. Any Templar can press the buttons; Cancel discards the draft, and drafts left untouched for 14 days are dropped.
- Added a competitions menu under the winners row. By default every competition ending next is ticked. The menu lists the group's competitions still to end, and ticking others moves the send time to 5 minutes after the last of them ends. With a typed `start` the send time stays as typed, the competitions ending next are still ticked, the menu lists every competition started by then (running ones included), and an Automatic option uses the ones that last ended by the send time.
- Routed the panel's buttons, competitions menu and pop-up through `src/handlers/eventHandler.js`.
- Verified the panel with discord.js's own component checks and scripted tests covering adding, editing, reordering and removing, the 5-announcement cap, a stale panel, an edit of a removed announcement, empty text, a non-Templar, the over-limit warning and cancelling. A full panel uses 39 of Discord's 40 components with the competitions menu. Verified the menu with scripted tests (Discord's builders and WOM simulated) covering the default, unticking, picking a later competition, picking across end times and Automatic, and its lists against the live WOM group.
- Added the weekly winners lists to `src/utils/weeklyAnnounce.js`. Competitions that end together are added into one list when they share a unit (kc with kc, xp with xp) and a title prefix, such as "BOTW T3 Gauntlet" and "BOTW T3 Corrupted Gauntlet", summing each player's gains. Others get their own list.

Tied players share a line and places follow the usual ranking (a tie for 2nd leaves no 3rd); a list where nobody gained says "Nobody gained any kc this week."
